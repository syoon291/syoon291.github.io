Nihongo Ears

Live2026
Problem
Living in Tokyo as a beginner, I needed to understand and answer everyday spoken Japanese, not read kanji. Most apps teach the opposite.
Built
An installable, offline phone app: audio-first flashcards in romaji, SM-2 spaced repetition, a shadowing mode that times your turn to speak, and a log for phrases used on real people.
Result
85 high-frequency spoken phrases and words to start, a daily routine I actually keep, and no account or server: progress stays on my phone.
